About QuantEdge

Founded by Akaash Deepak

QuantEdge is a student-led data analytics brand focused on quantitative research, visual storytelling, and clear conclusions using real-world datasets.

Mission

Turn real data into clear, useful insights — through honest methods, strong visuals, and evidence-backed conclusions.

What Makes QuantEdge Different

  • Every project starts with a specific question
  • Charts and visuals are explained, not just shown
  • Assumptions and limitations are stated clearly
  • Results are written like a short research paper

Research Process

1) Question

Define the exact problem and what success means.

2) Data

Find a dataset, verify it, and clean it.

3) Analysis

Explore trends, comparisons, and key metrics.

4) Visuals

Create charts that make the result obvious.

5) Conclusion

Write the takeaway and what it means in real life.
