Founded by Akaash Deepak

I am a high school student at Panther Creek High School with a strong passion for data analytics, research, and quantitative problem-solving. My interest in data extends beyond the classroom—through competitions like FBLA and independent projects, I have consistently explored how data can be used to uncover meaningful insights and drive informed decisions.

I am the founder of QuantEdge, a personal research initiative focused on transforming real-world datasets into clear, structured, and impactful analysis. My work centers on sports analytics, trend analysis, and data visualization, where I begin with a simple question and explore datasets using Python and spreadsheets.

Through QuantEdge, I am building a growing portfolio of independent research projects, with plans for future collaborations with professors and researchers. My goal is to demonstrate structured thinking, technical skill, and curiosity while developing a strong foundation for academic research and data-driven work.
